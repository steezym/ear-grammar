Body idioms for taking risks and dealing with annoying people.

1. Today's Idioms

Tekan 🔊 untuk mendengar, lalu tekan 🎤 dan ucapkan. Web akan memberi skor dan menandai kata yang belum jelas. (Mikrofon: pakai Chrome atau Edge, lalu izinkan akses mikrofon.)

🦒 stick one's neck out
take a risk to help someone or say what you think
berani mengambil risiko / pasang badan
Rina stuck her neck out and told the teacher the truth to defend me.
💇 get in someone's hair
annoy someone by always being around them
mengganggu, bikin kesal
My little brother keeps getting in my hair while I'm studying.
🎒 get off someone's back
stop criticising or pressuring someone
berhenti merecoki / menekan
I'll clean my room later. Please get off my back!
🦵 not have a leg to stand on
have no proof or good reason for what you say
tidak punya bukti / alasan kuat
He says I broke the window, but he doesn't have a leg to stand on. There's no proof.
💡 Tips: Ganti kata ganti sesuai orangnya: stick my neck out, get in her hair, get off his back. Get off my back terdengar kesal, jadi pakai dengan teman, bukan dengan guru.

E. Role-play (in class / voice note)

🎭 Situation: Kamu dituduh merusak laptop kelas, padahal bukan kamu pelakunya.
  1. Student A (teman sekelas): terus menuduh dan mengganggu (keeps getting in my hair).
  2. Student B (kamu): minta dia berhenti menekan (get off my back) dan bilang dia tidak punya bukti (you don't have a leg to stand on).
  3. Student C / guru (opsional): tanya siapa yang berani bersaksi.
  4. Student B: ceritakan teman yang membela kamu (stuck his/her neck out).

Target: percakapan 1–2 menit, semua 4 idiom terpakai. Bonus: pakai 1 idiom dari Sesi 1.
