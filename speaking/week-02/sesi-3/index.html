1. Today's Idioms

Tekan 🔊 untuk mendengar, lalu tekan 🎤 dan ucapkan. Web akan memberi skor dan menandai kata yang belum jelas. (Mikrofon: pakai Chrome atau Edge, lalu izinkan akses mikrofon.)

📢 shoot off one's mouth
talk loudly and carelessly, often boasting
asal ngomong / omong besar
He's always shooting off his mouth about how rich his family is.
😠 jump down someone's throat
suddenly answer someone very angrily
membentak tiba-tiba
I only asked a question! You don't have to jump down my throat.
👃 pay through the nose
pay far too much for something
bayar terlalu mahal
We paid through the nose for drinks at the airport.
😉 tongue-in-cheek
said as a joke, not seriously
bercanda, tidak serius
His review calling the movie a masterpiece was tongue-in-cheek. He actually hated it.
💡 Tips: Tongue-in-cheek adalah adjective/adverb: a tongue-in-cheek comment, he said it tongue-in-cheek. Pay through the nose sering dipakai dengan for: pay through the nose for tickets.

E. Role-play (in class / voice note)

🎭 Situation: Kamu dan temanmu baru pulang dari liburan singkat dan menceritakannya ke teman lain.
  1. Student A: ceritakan harga hotel / makanan yang sangat mahal (we paid through the nose).
  2. Student B: bercanda bahwa liburannya 'paling murah sedunia' lalu jelaskan itu bercanda (tongue-in-cheek).
  3. Student A: ceritakan teman seperjalanan yang suka omong besar (shooting off his/her mouth).
  4. Student B: ceritakan saat teman itu tiba-tiba membentak (jumped down my throat) dan bagaimana kalian berbaikan.

Target: percakapan 1–2 menit, semua 4 idiom terpakai. Bonus: pakai 2 idiom dari Sesi 1–2.
