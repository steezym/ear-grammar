1. Today's Idioms

Tekan 🔊 untuk mendengar, lalu tekan 🎤 dan ucapkan. Web akan memberi skor dan menandai kata yang belum jelas. (Mikrofon: pakai Chrome atau Edge, lalu izinkan akses mikrofon.)

🐂 take the bull by the horns
deal with a difficult situation bravely and directly
menghadapi masalah dengan berani
I was scared of public speaking, but I took the bull by the horns and joined the debate club.
🐴 straight from the horse's mouth
directly from the person who knows best
langsung dari sumbernya
The trip is cancelled. I heard it straight from the horse's mouth: our teacher told me herself.
🤸 horse around
play in a rough or silly way
main-main / bercanda berlebihan
Stop horsing around in the classroom! You'll break something.
🎨 a horse of a different color
a completely different matter
lain cerita / hal yang berbeda
You can borrow my pen, but my motorbike? That's a horse of a different color.
🐕 go to the dogs
become much worse than before
jadi rusak / memburuk
This park used to be beautiful, but it has gone to the dogs. There's rubbish everywhere.
💡 Tips: kata kerja di idiom berubah sesuai tense: I took the bull by the horns, they were horsing around, it has gone to the dogs. Straight from the horse's mouth biasanya dipakai setelah hear it / get it.

Tekan 🔊 untuk mendengar pertanyaan, lalu tekan 🎤 Jawab dengan suara dan jawab 2–3 kalimat memakai idiom hari ini. Ketuk lagi kalau sudah selesai; jawabanmu otomatis ditulis dan dicek. Bisa juga diketik manual.

E. Role-play (in class / voice note)

🎭 Situation: Kamu dan temanmu adalah pengurus klub sekolah. Ruang klub sudah lama tidak dirawat dan ada kabar klub akan ditutup.
  1. Student A: ceritakan kondisi ruang klub yang memburuk (go to the dogs) dan anggota yang suka main-main (horse around).
  2. Student B: tanya dari mana kabar penutupan itu (Did you hear it straight from the horse's mouth?).
  3. Student A: jawab, lalu usulkan untuk bertindak berani (take the bull by the horns).
  4. Student B: setuju membersihkan ruangan, tapi soal minta dana ke sekolah itu lain cerita (a horse of a different color).

Target: percakapan 1–2 menit, semua 5 idiom terpakai. Bonus: pakai juga 1 idiom dari Sesi 1 (smell a rat, fishy …).
