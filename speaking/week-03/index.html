1. Today's Idioms

Tekan 🔊 untuk mendengar, lalu tekan 🎤 dan ucapkan. Web akan memberi skor dan menandai kata yang belum jelas. (Mikrofon: pakai Chrome atau Edge, lalu izinkan akses mikrofon.)

🎣 feed someone a line
tell someone something untrue to trick them
membohongi / memberi alasan palsu
He said his phone was dead, but I think he was just feeding me a line.
🪀 string someone along
keep someone waiting with false promises
memberi harapan palsu
She keeps saying "maybe next week". I think she's stringing you along.
❄️ snow job
sweet talk or big lies used to fool someone
rayuan gombal / omong besar untuk menipu
The salesman's speech about the "magic" phone was a total snow job.
💡 Tips: feed someone a line dan string someone along sering dipakai dalam bentuk continuous: He's feeding you a line, She's stringing him along. Snow job adalah kata benda: That was a snow job.

E. Role-play (in class / voice note)

🎭 Situation: Temanmu sudah tiga kali membatalkan janji nongkrong dengan alasan yang aneh-aneh.
  1. Student A: ceritakan alasan anehmu ("my cat ate my phone charger…").
  2. Student B: bilang kamu rasa temanmu bohong (You're feeding me a line).
  3. Student A: janjikan "next week, for sure!".
  4. Student B: jawab bahwa dia terus memberi harapan palsu (Stop stringing me along) dan janjinya itu a snow job, lalu tentukan hari yang pasti.

Target: percakapan 1–2 menit, semua 3 idiom terpakai.
