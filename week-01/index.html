2. Grammar Focus

Kalau ada kata kerja setelah verb of preference, kata kerja itu pakai bentuk V-ing (gerund).

LevelPatternExample
😍 Very positivelove / adore + V-ingI love watching horror movies.
🙂 Positivelike / enjoy + V-ingShe enjoys cooking for her family.
😎 Casualbe into / be keen on / be fond of + V-ingI'm really into playing badminton.
😐 Neutraldon't mind + V-ingI don't mind washing the dishes.
🙁 Negativedon't like / dislike + V-ingHe doesn't like waking up early.
😫 Very negativehate / can't stand + V-ingI can't stand waiting in long queues.
Preposition + V-ing
Setelah in, at, of, about, kata kerja juga jadi V-ing.
  • I'm interested in learning Korean.
  • She's good at drawing anime.
  • I'm tired of scrolling social media.
Frequency adverbs
always › usually › often › sometimes › rarely › never
  • I usually go jogging on Sundays. (sebelum kata kerja)
  • He is never late for practice. (setelah to be)
⚠️ Common mistakes
I enjoy to read. → I enjoy reading.
I am liking playing games. → I like playing games.
I'm interested in play football. → I'm interested in playing football.
💡 Like + V-ing vs. Like + to V: dua-duanya benar, I like swimming / I like to swim. Tapi would like (= ingin) selalu + to V: I would like to try surfing.
📘 IELTS tip (Grammar for IELTS, Unit 2): like, love, want, know adalah stative verbs, jadi biasanya dipakai dalam simple tense: "I like studying Spanish in my free time", bukan "I am liking…".

3. -ing Spelling Rules

RuleExamples
Most verbs: + ingread → reading, play → playing
Silent -e: hapus e + ingdance → dancing, make → making
1 vowel + 1 consonant: double + ingswim → swimming, run → running, shop → shopping
-ie → y + inglie → lying, tie → tying
