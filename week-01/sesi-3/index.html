1. V-ing as the subject

Kegiatan (V-ing) bisa menjadi subjek kalimat. Kata kerja sesudahnya memakai bentuk tunggal: is, makes, helps, keeps.

Subject (V-ing)VerbRest of the sentence
Reading comicsismy favourite hobby.
Playing chessmakesme think carefully.
Listening to musichelpsme relax after school.
Running every morningkeepsme healthy.
⚠️ Common mistakes
Read is fun. → Reading is fun. (subjek harus V-ing, bukan V1)
Playing games are fun. → Playing games is fun. (satu kegiatan → is)

2. Preposition + V-ing

Setelah preposition (in, at, of, about, before, after, by, without, instead of …), kata kerja selalu V-ing.

Adjective + preposition
  • interested in learning Japanese
  • good / bad at drawing
  • fond of baking · keen on hiking
  • tired of waiting · excited about going
Other prepositions
  • I'm thinking about joining a band.
  • After finishing homework, I play guitar.
  • Before going to bed, I read a comic.
  • Let's go cycling instead of watching TV.
  • You can learn English by singing songs.
💡 Hati-hati: to di want to / plan to bukan preposition, jadi tetap to V1 (Sesi 2). Tapi look forward to adalah preposition: I look forward to seeing you.

3. Put it together: model paragraph

Semua pola Sesi 1–3 dalam satu paragraf. Bagian yang ditandai adalah pola yang bisa kamu pakai.

My Hobby
Drawing is my favourite hobby. I usually draw for an hour after dinner because it helps me relax. I enjoy creating anime characters, and I'm good at drawing faces. After finishing a drawing, I share it with my friends online. Next year, I want to learn digital art, and I hope to make my own comic one day.
