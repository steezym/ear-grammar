🔁 Quick review (Sesi 1)

Kemarin: like / love / enjoy + V-ing → I enjoy playing badminton. Hari ini kita belajar: kata kerja mana yang diikuti V-ing, mana yang diikuti to V, dan mana yang bisa dua-duanya.

1. Verb + V-ing

Kata kerja ini selalu diikuti V-ing. Biasanya tentang aktivitas yang sedang/sudah/sering dilakukan.

VerbExample (My Hobbies)
enjoyI enjoy drawing anime characters.
finishHave you finished reading that comic?
practiseShe practises singing every evening.
keep (= terus)He keeps playing games until midnight.
don't mindI don't mind waking up early for jogging.
avoidI avoid watching horror movies at night.
give up (= berhenti)My dad gave up smoking and started cycling.
suggestRina suggested going hiking on Sunday.

2. Verb + to V

Kata kerja ini selalu diikuti to + V1. Biasanya tentang keinginan, rencana, atau harapan (masa depan).

VerbExample (My Hobbies)
wantI want to learn the guitar.
would likeShe'd like to join the dance club.
planWe plan to go camping next month.
hopeHe hopes to win the chess competition.
decideI decided to start a YouTube channel.
learnMy sister learned to swim when she was six.
needYou need to practise every day.
promiseHe promised to teach me photography.
💡 Cara cepat mengingat: V-ing → aktivitasnya sudah/sedang terjadi (enjoy, finish, keep). to V → aktivitasnya belum terjadi, masih rencana atau keinginan (want, plan, hope).

3. Both: to V or V-ing

✅ Same meaning
like, love, hate, prefer, start, begin, continue
  • I like swimming = I like to swim.
  • It started raining = It started to rain.
⚠️ Different meaning
  • stop playing = berhenti main
    stop to buy a drink = berhenti untuk beli minum
  • remember meeting = ingat pernah
    remember to bring = ingat untuk (jangan lupa)
  • try turning it off = coba (sebagai percobaan)
    try to finish = berusaha
⚠️ Common mistakes
I want learning Korean. → I want to learn Korean.
She decided joining the club. → She decided to join the club.
Do you mind to wait? → Do you mind waiting?
