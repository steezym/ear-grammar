I've Been Learning Guitar!

Free Time: present perfect continuous for activities that started in the past and are still going on.

I've been playing · How long have you been…? · for / since
🅰️ When? 🅱️ Form ⚖️ vs present perfect 🎯 Practice 🏆 Result

1. When do we use it?

Dari Grammar for IELTS Unit 3: aksi yang dimulai di masa lalu dan masih berlangsung / baru saja selesai.

UseExample (free time)
1. Started in the past, still continuing
masih berlangsung sampai sekarang		I've been learning the guitar for six months.
2. How long?
menanyakan durasi		How long have you been playing badminton?
3. Recent activity with a result now
baru selesai, hasilnya terlihat		I'm tired because I've been cycling all morning.
⏳ for + durasi
for two hours · for a week · for three years · for ages
📍 since + titik waktu
since 2022 · since Monday · since 7 a.m. · since I was a kid

2. Form: have / has + been + V-ing

I / you / we / theyhe / she / it
+They've been playing.She's been playing.
–They haven't been playing.She hasn't been playing.
?Have they been playing?Has she been playing?

3. Present perfect continuous vs present perfect

🔄 have been + V-ing → prosesnya / berapa lama
  • I've been reading this novel for two weeks. (belum selesai)
  • She's been baking all day. (fokus aktivitas)
✅ have + V3 → hasilnya / berapa banyak
  • I've read three novels this month. (sudah selesai, jumlah)
  • She's baked 50 cookies. (hasil)
⚠️ I'm learning English since 2020 → I've been learning English since 2020 · I've been knowing her → I've known her (state verb)
