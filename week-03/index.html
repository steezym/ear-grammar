Present continuousExample (free time)

3. Form: am / is / are + V-ing

Ihe / she / ityou / we / they
+I'm playing.She's playing.They're playing.
–I'm not playing.She isn't playing.They aren't playing.
?Am I playing?Is she playing?Are they playing?
🚫 State verbs (tidak pakai -ing)
  • feeling: like, love, hate, want, prefer
  • thinking: know, understand, believe, remember
  • having: have (= punya), own, belong
  • I'm liking this song → I like this song
⚠️ Common mistakes
  • I playing games now → I'm playing
  • She is play → She is playing
  • I'm play every day → I play every day
  • Spelling: swim → swimming, ride → riding, lie → lying
