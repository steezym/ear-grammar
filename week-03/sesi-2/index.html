I Was Gaming When…

Free Time: past continuous for actions in progress in the past.

I was playing · They were watching · when / while
🅰️ When? 🅱️ Form ⏱️ when / while 🎯 Practice 🏆 Result

1. When do we use the past continuous?

Dari Grammar for IELTS Unit 2: aksi yang sedang berlangsung di masa lalu.

UseExample (free time)
1. In progress at a past time
sedang terjadi pada jam tertentu		At 9 p.m. last night I was watching a horror film.
2. Interrupted action
aksi panjang dipotong aksi pendek		I was playing a game when the power went off.
3. Two actions at the same time
dua aksi bersamaan		While Mum was cooking, Dad was cleaning the garden.
4. Background of a story
latar cerita		The sun was shining and birds were singing…

2. Form: was / were + V-ing

I / he / she / ityou / we / they
+She was reading.We were reading.
–She wasn't reading.We weren't reading.
?Was she reading?Were you reading?

3. when + past simple · while + past continuous

⏱️ when → aksi pendek (past simple)
  • I was jogging when it started to rain.
  • When my friend called, I was sleeping.
⏳ while → aksi panjang (past continuous)
  • While I was jogging, it started to rain.
  • I fell asleep while I was watching TV.
⚠️ I was knowing him → I knew him (state verb). · They was playing → They were playing.
