Sentence Building

1like / love / enjoy + V-ing

  • I love listening to music.
  • She enjoys cooking for her family.

2don't mind / hate / can't stand + V-ing

  • I don't mind washing the dishes.
  • I can't stand waiting in queues.

3interested in / good at + V-ing

  • I'm interested in learning Korean.
  • She's good at drawing.

4usually / often / sometimes + V

  • I usually go jogging on Sundays.
  • We sometimes play chess.

5would like + to V

  • I would like to try surfing.
  • He'd like to learn the piano.
⚠️ Watch out!

I enjoy to read. → I enjoy reading.

I am liking games. → I like games.

interested in play → interested in playing

swiming, danceing → swimming, dancing
