Use It!

1Something feels wrong

A: He's being extra nice to me today.
B: Hmm, I smell a rat!

2A suspicious message

A: "You won a free phone! Send your PIN."
B: Don't! That's fishy.

3Oops, the surprise!

A: How does Dina know about the party?
B: Rudi let the cat out of the bag.

4A quiet friend

A: You haven't said a word all day.
Cat got your tongue?

5A boring meeting

A: Three hours and nothing decided!
B: Meetings like that are for the birds.

⚠️ Watch out!

This soup is fishy, yum! → fishy = suspicious

Bali was amazing, for the birds! → = boring

I smell a rat yesterday. → I smelled a rat

She letted the cat out. → She let the cat out
