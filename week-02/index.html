2. When do we use the present simple?

Dari Grammar for IELTS Unit 1, ada 4 pemakaian utama:

UseExample (travel)
1. Permanent situations
keadaan tetap		I live in Bogor, but I study in Jakarta.
2. Habits / repeated actions
kebiasaan		We visit my grandma in Yogyakarta every Lebaran.
3. Facts
fakta umum / sains		Bali attracts millions of tourists. Planes use a lot of fuel.
4. Timetables
jadwal		The train to Bandung leaves at 8 a.m. The museum opens at 9.
💡 Time words: every day / week / summer · usually · often · sometimes · on Sundays · once a year · twice a month

3. Form

I / you / we / theyhe / she / it
+I travel by bus.She travels by bus.
–I don't travel by plane.She doesn't travel by plane.
?Do you travel often?Does she travel often?
-s / -es spelling
  • most verbs + s: visit → visits, stay → stays
  • -ch, -sh, -s, -x, -o + es: watch → watches, go → goes, fix → fixes
  • consonant + y → ies: fly → flies, study → studies
  • have → has
⚠️ Common mistakes
  • She travel → She travels
  • He don't like → He doesn't like
  • She doesn't likes → She doesn't like
  • Does he goes? → Does he go?
