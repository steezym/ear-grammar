2. When do we use the present perfect?

Dari Grammar for IELTS Unit 1:

UseExampleKey words
1. Started in the past, continues nowI have lived in Depok for five years / since 2021.for, since
2. Experience in an unfinished time (life, this year)Have you ever been abroad? I've visited three cities this year.ever, never, this year
3. Recent events with a result nowOh no, I've lost my passport! We've just arrived.just, already, yet
for + lama waktu
for two days · for a week · for ten years
since + titik waktu mulai
since Monday · since 2020 · since I was ten

3. Present perfect vs past simple

Present perfect: kapan tidak disebut / waktu belum selesai
  • I've been to Bali twice.
  • She has already packed her bag.
Past simple: kapan disebut / waktu sudah selesai
  • I went to Bali last year.
  • She packed her bag yesterday.
⚠️ Common mistakes
I have visited Bali last year. → I visited Bali last year.
She has went to Paris. → She has gone / been to Paris.
I live here since 2020. → I have lived here since 2020.
🗣️ IELTS Speaking tip: mulai dengan present perfect, lalu beri detail dengan past simple. "Yes, I've been to Lombok. I went there last year and I climbed Mount Rinjani."
