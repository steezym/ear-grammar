Holidays and Travel: past simple for finished trips and experiences.

I visited · I went · I didn't go
🅰️ When? 🅱️ -ed 🔀 Irregular 🎯 Practice 🏆 Result

1. When do we use the past simple?

Dari Grammar for IELTS Unit 1, past simple dipakai untuk:

UseExample (travel)
1. Finished actions
kejadian yang sudah selesai		I visited the Borobudur temple.
2. A specific time in the past
waktunya jelas		We went to Lombok last month. She flew to Japan in 2023.
3. Past habits
kebiasaan dulu		When I was a kid, we always spent Lebaran in my grandma's village.
💡 Time words: yesterday · last week / month / year · two days ago · in 2022 · when I was ten

2. Form: regular verbs (-ed)

RuleExamples
most verbs + edvisit → visited, stay → stayed, walk → walked
ends in -e → + darrive → arrived, dance → danced
consonant + y → iedtry → tried, study → studied
1 vowel + 1 consonant → doublestop → stopped, plan → planned
– Negative
I didn't + V1
  • We didn't stay at a hotel.
  • She didn't go swimming.
? Question
Did + subject + V1?
  • Did you enjoy the trip?
  • Where did you stay?

3. Irregular verbs for travelling

go → went
see → saw
take → took
eat → ate
buy → bought
fly → flew
swim → swam
have → had
get → got
leave → left
spend → spent
come → came
⚠️ Common mistakes
I didn't went. → I didn't go. (setelah didn't → V1)
Did you saw it? → Did you see it?
We goed / She buyed → We went / She bought
